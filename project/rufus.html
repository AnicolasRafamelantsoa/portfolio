Présentation du projet

Rufous Ecotravel is an adventure-focused travel agency dedicated to exploring the world beyond the ordinary. We design unique, experience-rich journeys that immerse travelers in nature, culture, and authentic local life. From mountain treks and jungle hikes to water adventures and wildlife encounters, Rufous Ecotravel curates unforgettable trips for all levels of adventurers. Each itinerary is thoughtfully crafted with a strong commitment to safety, sustainability, and personalization.
